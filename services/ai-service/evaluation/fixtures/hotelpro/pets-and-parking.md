# Pets, parking and accessibility

## Pets

Dogs and cats up to 20 kilograms are welcome in pet-friendly rooms on the ground floor. The charge is 15 euros per pet per night, with a maximum of two pets per room. Pets are not allowed in the restaurant or the pool area.

## Parking

The underground car park costs 22 euros per night and has 6 electric vehicle charging points. Spaces cannot be reserved and are offered first come, first served. The car park has a height limit of 2 metres.

## Accessibility

Eight rooms are wheelchair accessible, with roll-in showers and lowered fittings. All public areas can be reached by lift. Assistance dogs stay free of charge in any room.

## Smoking

The hotel is entirely non-smoking, including balconies. A cleaning charge of 150 euros applies if smoking is detected in a room.

## Lost property

Items left behind are kept for 3 months. We can post them back to you at your own cost.
