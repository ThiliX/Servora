# Check-in and facilities

## Check-in and check-out times

Check-in starts at 3 pm and check-out is by 11 am. Reception is staffed 24 hours a day.

## Early check-in and late check-out

Early check-in from 11 am costs 20 euros, when a room is ready. Late check-out until 2 pm costs 30 euros. Both must be requested in advance.

## Breakfast

A buffet breakfast is served in the Garden Room from 6.30 am to 10.30 am, and until 11 am at weekends. It costs 18 euros per adult and is free for children under six.

## Pool and gym

The indoor pool is open from 7 am to 9 pm. The gym is open around the clock with your room key. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult in the pool.

## Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is free throughout the hotel. A faster premium connection for video calls and streaming costs 8 euros per day.

## Airport shuttle

A shuttle runs between the hotel and the airport every 30 minutes from 5 am to 11 pm. It costs 6 euros per person each way.
