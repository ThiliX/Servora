# Booking and cancellation

## Free cancellation

Flexible rate bookings can be cancelled free of charge until 48 hours before the arrival date. After that, the first night is charged.

## Non-refundable rate

Saver rate bookings are paid in full at the time of booking and cannot be refunded or cancelled. The dates can be moved once, for a fee of 25 euros, up to 7 days before arrival.

## Changing a booking

You can change dates or room type in Manage booking on our website, or by calling reception. Changes depend on availability and the price may differ.

## No-shows

Guests who do not arrive and have not cancelled are charged the full stay.

## Deposits

A security deposit of 100 euros per room is held on your card at check-in and released within 5 working days after check-out.

## Group bookings

Reservations of six rooms or more are handled by our groups team and need a signed contract.
