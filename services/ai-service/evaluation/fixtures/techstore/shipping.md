# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery arrives the next business day when you order before 2 pm.

## Shipping costs

Standard delivery is free on orders over 50 euros and costs 4.99 euros otherwise. Express delivery costs 12.99 euros on every order.

## Where we deliver

We deliver to all European Union countries, Norway and Switzerland. We do not ship to PO boxes or freight forwarders.

## Tracking your order

A tracking link is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse. You can also find it under Orders in your account.

## Damaged or missing parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, or tracking shows delivered but you have nothing, contact us within 14 days of the delivery date so we can open a claim with the courier.
