# Warranty and repairs

## Standard warranty

Every new device sold by TechStore includes a 2 year warranty against manufacturing defects. Refurbished devices include a 12 month warranty.

## What the warranty does not cover

Accidental damage such as cracked screens and liquid spills is not covered. Neither is normal battery wear, or damage caused by repairs from unauthorised workshops.

## Extended protection plan

TechStore Care adds accidental damage cover and extends the warranty to 4 years in total. It must be purchased within 60 days of buying the device.

## Getting a repair

Open a repair request from your account. We collect the device by courier free of charge. Most repairs are completed within 10 business days, and we lend you a replacement laptop for repairs that take longer.

## Data and repairs

Back up your data before sending a device in. Storage may be erased during repair and TechStore is not responsible for lost files.
