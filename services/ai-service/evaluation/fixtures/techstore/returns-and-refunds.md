# Returns and refunds

## Return window

You can return most products within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Opened software, gift cards and custom-built computers cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

## How to start a return

Sign in to your TechStore account, open Orders, and choose Return item. We email you a prepaid shipping label within one business day. Drop the parcel at any partner courier point.

## Refund timing

Once the returned item reaches our warehouse we inspect it within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.

## Restocking fee

Items returned without the original packaging are charged a 15 percent restocking fee. There is no fee for faulty items.

## Exchanges

We do not swap items directly. To exchange a product, return it for a refund and place a new order.
